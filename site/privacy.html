Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026
Developer: Ambrózy Lőrinc

MyNewPage is a new tab page for Chrome (the "extension") and a matching web page at https://mynewpage.as.hu/myhome/index.html (the "web version"). This policy explains what data they handle. In short: your favorites stay in your own Google Drive, and the developer does not receive, store or see your data.

What the app stores

  • Your favorites: the titles and web addresses (URLs) of the favorites you add, the folders you create, and their order.
  • Your settings: your chosen interface language.

This data is not personal information that the developer collects. It is kept in two places that you control:

  1. Your Google Drive. After you sign in with Google, the data is saved as a single file (favorites.json) in the hidden application data folder (appDataFolder) of your own Google Drive. Only MyNewPage, signed in as you, can read this folder. It does not use your other Drive files, and it does not appear in your normal Drive view.
  2. Your browser. A local copy is stored in your browser (chrome.storage.local in the extension, localStorage in the web version) so that the page loads instantly and works offline. In the web version, the temporary Google access token is also kept in localStorage until it expires (about one hour).

Google sign-in and permissions

Signing in is optional. Without signing in, your favorites are stored only in your browser and are not synced.

When you sign in, MyNewPage asks for exactly one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata ("View and manage its own configuration data in your Google Drive"). It is used only to read and write the favorites.json file described above. MyNewPage does not request your name, email address, contacts or any other Google data.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What the developer collects

Nothing. MyNewPage has no developer-operated servers or databases for user data, no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no cookies set by the developer. Your data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The data is never transferred to or processed on any server other than Google's, as described below.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Sign out: in the settings (gear icon), choose "Sign out". This revokes the access token and deletes the local copy from your browser. The file in your Drive is kept.
  • Delete your synced data: open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, find MyNewPage and choose "Delete hidden app data". You can also edit or delete individual favorites in the app at any time.
  • Revoke access: remove MyNewPage in your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps & services.
  • Uninstall: removing the extension deletes its local data from the browser.

Open source

The source code of both the extension and the web version is public, so you can verify what they do: github.com/Meister1977/MyNewPage.

Children

MyNewPage is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.
